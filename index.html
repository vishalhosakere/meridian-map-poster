Meridian — Map Poster Studio

Meridian turns any city into a printable, fully re-colourable map poster. Search for a city, neighbourhood or address and Meridian draws its roads, railways, rivers, coastline, parks and buildings from OpenStreetMap data, directly on a canvas in your browser. There are no accounts, API keys or tile servers involved.

  • Any place on Earth, from 0.5 to 50 km across, with optional railways, footpaths and buildings.
  • 26 themes, including Ink & Paper, Blueprint, Noir, Gold Leaf and Neon Noir, plus a colour control for every map layer.
  • Framed or edge-to-edge posters with titles and coordinates, circle crops and seven aspect ratios from 1:1 to 16:9.
  • Drag, pinch and rotate the map on desktop or phone, then export a high-resolution PNG up to 8K.
